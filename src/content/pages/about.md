---
title: "About Erika Lacroix"
headline: "Explorations of physical texture, quiet geometry, and layered history."
---

Erika Lacroix is an artist working primarily in acrylic and cold wax medium on birch panel.

Her process emphasizes tactile surfaces, iterative layering, and subtractive scraping—revealing undercurrents of color and organic forms that mirror natural weathering and architectural time.
